A few little questions

Before you wander in.

Do I have to be on a big trip to use Roam?

Not at all. A walk through a new neighborhood, a weekend away, or a familiar place seen differently can be a story worth keeping.

What belongs in my travel diary?

Whatever helps you remember the moment: a photograph, a few words, a place, or a little detail you don’t want to lose.

What’s the difference between journeys and saved places?

Journeys bring your travel memories together. Saved places hold the destinations and discoveries you’d like to come back to someday.
